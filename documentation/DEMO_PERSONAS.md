# Demo personas

Seven fictional Pax clients for the pitch and for testing new features. `pnpm db:reset` seeds
them next to Jane, Lily and Taylor. Each one has a profile and policies, one or two earlier
conversations with their analysis, and, where it fits, leads, handovers, announced future
events and e-mails. All dates are relative to the day of the seed, so the follow-ups stay ahead.

Each persona exists three times, with the same facts:

- **Seed** (`code/scripts/seed-personas.ts`): what the company dashboard and the cockpit show.
- **Simulated customer** (`code/src/lib/simulation/demo-personas.ts`): backstory, goals,
  worries, speaking style and what they reveal when. To run one or more:
  `POST /api/simulation` with `{ "personas": ["student_first_job", "self_employed_8165"] }`
  (needs the advisor cookie).
- **Scripted chat** (`code/scripts/persona-scenarios.ts`): beats and the expected advisor
  behaviour, in the format of the converse harness (`scripts/converse.ts`).

Start the customer side at `/kunde` ("Ich bin bereits Pax-Kunde") and choose the persona. The
company side is `/firma` (any advisor). The time-travel control is at the top of
`/firma/wiedervorlagen`. "Demo zurücksetzen" removes the generated follow-up leads and e-mails.

## Follow-ups

- Real PDFs for the personas are not generated yet (`scripts/sample-docs.ts` is rewritten on
  feat/vorsorgebild-demo-docs). Useful next: a Vorsorgeausweis for Hans-Peter Wyss (the
  figures above) and for Selin Aydin, and the Pax term life policy of Camille Rochat.

  is pending and may create a second one (Andrin, Hans-Peter, Camille).

  longer lead time would be more useful.
