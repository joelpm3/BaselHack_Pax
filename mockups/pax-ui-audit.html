Pax UI audit

Where the Clara app departs from the Pax brand guide (documentation/challenge_context/BRAND.md, DESIGN.md) and Pax's own UI references. Screenshots from the local app on 31.10.2026, ordered by how visible each issue is.

High

1. Next-step card: accent bar bent around a rounded corner

Pax rule
Pax uses Light Purple as a plain info box; no other place in the app combines a left accent bar with large rounded corners.
Where
code/src/app/[locale]/firma/(app)/kunden/[id]/page.tsx:134 · rounded-xl border-l-4, plus underline on hover for the whole card
Fix
Drop the bar and keep the rounded Light Purple box, with a small arrow for the link; or keep a straight 2px bar on white like the other accent lines.
High

2. «Live» indicator floating in the top-right corner

Pax rule
Nothing like it exists in the Pax UI: a lone pulsing green square with grey text, outside any header or badge, reads like leftover debug output.
Where
code/src/components/firma/live-events.tsx:136 (LiveStatus, animate-pulse bg-pax-green) · shown top right on kunden/[id]/page.tsx:101, gespraeche/[id]/page.tsx:96, and in the headers of /firma/live and /firma/simulation
Fix
Drop it from the customer and conversation pages (they refresh themselves anyway); on /firma/live show it as a proper badge next to the title, or only when the connection is lost.
High

3. Headlines are purple, black or white instead of Pax Green

Pax rule
BRAND.md: «Für Headlines wird Pax Green verwendet.» On light backgrounds use Contrast Green for legibility. Pax's own website, portal and forms do this (reference below).
Where
Home and /kunde: text-pax-purple h1s · Vorsorge-Cockpit hero «Guten Tag» white on purple · section headings («Hinweise für Sie», dashboard titles) black
Fix
Hero headlines on purple in Pax Green; headlines on white in Contrast Green (or keep purple deliberately and say so in the pitch).
Medium

4. Primary buttons are purple; Pax CTAs are green pills

Pax rule
Pax UI reference: primary CTA is a Pax Green pill with dark text («Jetzt online berechnen», «Lösung finden», «Senden»), secondary is an outline pill.
Where
code/src/components/ui/button.tsx:8 · primary: bg-pax-purple text-white (the green look exists as inverse) · e.g. «Anmelden», «Zur Beratung»
Fix
Make the green pill the primary CTA, at least on purple surfaces and for the main action per screen.
Medium

5. No Freisteller imagery anywhere

Pax rule
BRAND.md: digital surfaces are illustrated with Freisteller (one object on Pax Purple with a #500099 halo) or Flächenbilder; Pax heroes all carry one.
Where
Home hero and the Cockpit hero use text and squares only.
Fix
One AI-generated Freisteller per hero, made with the prompt template in BRAND.md (e.g. a pram for «Familie», a rocket for «Start»).
Medium

6. Native browser selects in the dashboard filters

Pax rule
Pax forms use the UI library's fields (border, 10px radius, Pax chevron); system selects look different on every OS.
Where
Leads filters and the status/assignee dropdowns in the lead rows (/firma/leads)
Fix
Style select once in the shared Field/Select component (appearance-none, Lucide ChevronDown, Pax border and radius).
Low

7. Tailwind default text sizes instead of the Pax type scale

Pax rule
DESIGN.md §2.2: paragraph-xs/s/m and heading-s/m/l/xl.
Where
102 uses of text-xs/sm/base/2xl in 34 files, mostly the company dashboard (sections.tsx, leads-section.tsx, rules-editor.tsx, leads-kanban.tsx …); some labels end up at 11–12px
Fix
Map xs→paragraph-xs, sm→paragraph-s, base→paragraph-m, 2xl→heading-m in one pass.
Low

8. Italic signature field

Pax rule
BRAND.md: Pax Civil Bold/Regular/Medium only, no Italic.
Where
code/src/components/widgets/sales/offer-widget.tsx:455 · text-heading-s italic
Fix
Remove italic; the pen icon and the line already say «signature».
Low

9. Icon stroke widths other than 2px

Pax rule
BRAND.md: Lucide, colour #121212, stroke 2px, 24px.
Where
strokeWidth 1.75 in firma-shell.tsx:79 (sidebar), talk-dock.tsx:161 (mic), kind-mark.tsx:34, firma/(app)/page.tsx:179; 2.5 in widgets/sales/shared.tsx:144
Fix
Drop the overrides.
Low

10. Hard-coded 16px radius instead of the radius token

Pax rule
DESIGN.md §4.2: rounded-xl is 16px on mobile and 20px on desktop.
Where
rounded-[16px] in 21 files (portal, chat, widgets, ui/card.tsx); rounded-[8px] in firma/page-parts.tsx
Fix
Replace with rounded-xl / rounded-m.

Already on-brand

  • Logo: positive on white, negative (green + white dot) on purple
  • Colours all come from Pax tokens; no raw hex in components
  • No gradients, decorative shadows, uppercase or SemiBold
  • Green text on white uses Contrast Green (links, «Aus dem Gespräch …»)
  • Login: Pax Green headline on Deep Purple, Deep Purple navigation sidebar
  • Yellow accent only as a «Demo» Störer badge
